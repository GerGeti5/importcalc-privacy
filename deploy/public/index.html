Why a shipping calculator matters before checkout

International shopping often begins with a simple question: is the item cheaper online than it is locally? The answer is not always visible on the product page. A store may show a low product price, but the real landed cost can include domestic delivery to a warehouse, international forwarding, billable parcel weight, handling rules, currency conversion and customs-related expenses. ImportCalc Pro was built to make this planning step easier for shoppers in Georgia.

The website and Android app are designed for practical, pre-purchase estimates. Instead of guessing after the parcel arrives, users can enter the item price, expected weight and delivery tariff, then compare the result with other carriers or routes. This is useful for everyday purchases, discounted products, electronics, shoes, clothing, accessories and heavier household items where shipping can change the decision.
