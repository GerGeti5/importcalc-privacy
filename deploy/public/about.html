About ImportCalc Pro

ImportCalc Pro is an informational calculator and guide for online shoppers in Georgia.

Our purpose

ImportCalc Pro was created to make international online shopping more predictable for users in Georgia. The goal is simple: help shoppers understand the approximate cost before placing an order. Many people discover the real cost only after the parcel arrives.

The Android app and website provide calculation tools, route-based thinking and practical guides about parcel weight, delivery tariffs, shipping companies and customs-related planning.

What the project is not

ImportCalc Pro is not a carrier, customs broker, financial adviser or official government source. It does not guarantee the final cost of a shipment. Instead, it helps users create a useful estimate and compare scenarios.
