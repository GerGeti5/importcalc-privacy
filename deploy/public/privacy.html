Privacy Policy

For the ImportCalc Pro Android app and website.

Effective date: September 3, 2026

ImportCalc Pro helps users estimate import, shipping, customs-related, and profit calculations. This policy explains how information is handled by the ImportCalc Pro Android app and the website at importcalc.web.app.

Information handled by the Android app

The app does not require account registration and does not ask for a name, email address, or phone number. Values entered for calculations are used to produce calculation results and are not sold by the developer.

The app uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share approximate location derived from an IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information, and device or other identifiers. This information may be used for advertising or marketing, analytics, and fraud prevention, security, and compliance. Data transmitted by the SDK is encrypted in transit.

Website, advertising, and contact

The website does not require an account. Supported-language pages may use Google AdSense. Google and its partners may process IP address, browser or device information, cookies or similar technologies, and interaction data to provide, personalize, secure, and measure advertising according to user settings and applicable policies.

If you contact us by email, we use your email address and message only to respond and to handle the request.

Third-party services

Google processes information under its own policies. Review the Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising technologies information.

Security and retention

We take reasonable steps to keep the app and website secure. The developer does not operate an ImportCalc user-account database. Information processed by Google is retained and deleted according to Google's policies and user controls.

Children's privacy

ImportCalc Pro is not specifically directed to children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will publish the revised effective date on this page. Questions about this policy can be sent to maimunicoba@gmail.com.
