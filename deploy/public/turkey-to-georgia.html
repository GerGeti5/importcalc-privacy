Turkey to Georgia shipping cost guide

Practical cost planning for shoppers ordering from Turkey.

Why Turkey is a common route

Turkey is popular for clothing, shoes, accessories, household goods, beauty products and marketplace purchases. Delivery can be relatively fast compared with many distant routes, but final cost still depends on parcel weight, carrier tariff, warehouse handling and possible additional procedures.

The biggest risk is a basket that looks cheap item by item but becomes expensive when all parcels are combined. Clothing and shoes can also create size and return issues.

What to calculate before checkout

  • Total basket value, not only one discounted item.
  • Expected parcel weight after packaging.
  • Carrier tariff for Turkey-to-Georgia delivery.
  • Whether the product is bulky, boxed or fragile.
