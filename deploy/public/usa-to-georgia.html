USA to Georgia shipping cost guide

What to check before ordering from American stores and marketplaces.

Why the USA route is popular

The United States is popular for electronics, clothing, shoes, cosmetics, accessories, books, outlet deals and products that are difficult to find locally. Many shoppers use forwarding warehouses because some American stores do not ship directly to Georgia.

A typical USA order can include the item price, sales tax in some cases, domestic delivery to the warehouse, international shipping by weight and possible customs-related expenses. A sale price may be attractive, but the order can become less profitable when the parcel is heavy, oversized or split into multiple packages.

Checklist before buying from the USA

  • Check whether the seller offers free domestic delivery.
  • Estimate the shipping weight with packaging.
  • Save the invoice and order confirmation.
  • Compare the total cost with local Georgian prices.

Example planning scenario

If an item costs $120 and the expected parcel weight is 2.5 kg with an $8/kg tariff, international shipping alone is about $20. That means the order is already at least $140 before any other costs.
