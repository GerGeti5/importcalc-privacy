China to Georgia shipping cost guide

How to think about cost, weight and delivery route before ordering from Chinese marketplaces.

What makes China orders different

Orders from China are often small, frequent and price-sensitive. Product prices can be low, but shoppers should still consider shipping method, consolidation, delivery time, seller reliability and package tracking. Many users order several inexpensive items and only later discover that separate parcels or slow delivery make the experience less convenient than expected.

Consolidation and multiple parcels

One of the main questions for China orders is whether items arrive separately or can be consolidated. Consolidation can reduce handling effort in some cases, but it can also create a larger parcel. A larger parcel can change volumetric weight or make the package less convenient.

  • Check whether each seller ships separately.
  • Track all parcels carefully.
  • Keep invoices for each order.
  • Consider the final combined cost.
